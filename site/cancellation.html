Cancellation Policy

There is nothing to pay for, so there is nothing to cancel. This page says so plainly, and sets out your rights for the day that changes.

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Today, in full. FoitiFinder is free. There are no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no in-app purchases, and no paid super likes — the super like allowance renews on its own and cannot be bought. You have no payment details with us because we have never asked for any, and we take no payments at all. "Cancelling" means deleting your account, which you can do from inside the app in under a minute, with no notice period and no reason required.

Ending your use of FoitiFinder

You can stop at any time, and there are two different things you might mean by it.

Stop being shown to people, but keep your account
Turn match notifications off in the app and stop opening it. Your profile stays in the deck while your account exists, so if you want to be out of circulation entirely, delete it — at the moment there is no separate "pause" switch.
Leave for good
Delete your account. Your profile, photos, swipes and matches go with it. There is no fee, no grace period to wait out, and nothing to confirm by email.

Deleting your account ends the User Terms between us. It does not take back an Instagram handle you have already shared with somebody — nothing can, and the Terms explain why.

If we ever charge for something

We may one day offer a paid feature. If we do, we will say so clearly before you buy anything, and the following will apply. None of it applies today, because there is nothing to buy.

Your 14-day right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the EU you have 14 days from the purchase of digital content or a digital service to withdraw from it and get your money back, under the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU), implemented in Greece by Law 2251/1994 as amended. You do not need a reason.

There is one catch worth knowing in advance: if you ask us to start supplying a digital service immediately and acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once it is fully performed, that right can fall away. We will set out any such consent explicitly at the point of purchase rather than burying it, and for anything that runs over time — a month of something — you would get a refund in proportion to what was left.

How a cancellation would work

  • Anything bought through Google Play is billed by Google, not by us. You would manage and cancel the subscription in the Play Store (Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions), and refunds would go through Google's refund process. We cannot see your payment details or issue a refund on your card ourselves.
  • Cancelling would stop the renewal, not the current period. You would keep what you paid for until it runs out, and would not be charged again.
  • Deleting your account would not automatically cancel a subscription bought through Google Play, because that contract is with Google. Cancel there first, then delete.
  • To exercise the 14-day right against us directly, writing to [[EMAIL]] and saying so would be enough. No form.

If we withdraw a paid feature

We would refund the unused part of anything you had paid for. We would not quietly take away something you were paying for and keep the money.

Complaints

Write to [[EMAIL]] first. If we cannot sort it out, the User Terms list the consumer dispute bodies open to you, including the Hellenic Consumer Ombudsman.
