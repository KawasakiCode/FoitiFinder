Privacy Statement

FoitiFinder is a dating app, which means almost everything in it is personal data about you. This explains all of it.

Last updated 9 November 2026.

The short version. We hold what you put on your profile, who you swiped and how, your matches, and the device token that lets us send you a notification. We do not track your location, we run no advertising and no analytics, and we sell nothing to anybody. Other users see your profile; nobody but you sees your Instagram handle until you and one other person have each said yes twice. You can delete all of it from inside the app.

3. Sexual orientation, religion and political views

Article 9 of the GDPR treats these as special category data and forbids processing them unless a narrow condition applies. The one we rely on is your explicit consent — Article 9(2)(a).

In practice that means:

  • All three fields are optional, and an account with all three blank works exactly like any other.
  • If you fill one in, it goes on your profile where other users can see it. That is the only thing we do with it. Nothing filters or ranks on these fields.
  • You withdraw consent by clearing the field, at any time, from your profile. The value is removed from our database, and withdrawing has no other effect on your account.

Your gender and pronouns are not special category data under Article 9, but we recognise they can be just as sensitive. Pronouns are optional. Gender is not, because gender filtering is how the app decides who to show you at all.

4. Who can see what

Other users

Your profile is visible to other signed-in FoitiFinder users whose preferences you fit and who have not already swiped past you. It is not visible on the public internet, to search engines, or to anyone without a verified account at a Greek public university.

Your Instagram handle is the exception to all of that. It is handed over only when every one of the following has happened: you liked each other, and then each of you separately confirmed the match on the match page. Until the second confirmation lands, neither side has the other's handle. This is enforced on our server rather than in the app, so a modified app cannot get at it early.

A handle cannot be un-shared. Once it has been revealed, removing the match hides it from your own list but does not take it back from the other person — and the app tells you so before you confirm.

Us

We can read what is in the database when we have a reason to: fixing a fault, or looking into a report. We do not read it out of interest.

Nobody else

We disclose personal data outside the service only where we are legally obliged to — a lawful request from a competent authority — or where it is necessary to protect someone's life or safety. A report concerning a minor or a sexual offence may be referred to the authorities.

5. Your photos

Photos are uploaded to Firebase Storage (Google) under a path tied to your account identifier. The app then holds a download link to each one.

Those links are long and unguessable, but they are not individually access-controlled: anybody who has the link to one of your photos can open it, with or without a FoitiFinder account, for as long as the file exists. This is how the images load quickly in the app. Treat a photo you put on a dating profile as a photo that can travel — which is true of every app of this kind, screenshots included. Deleting the photo, or your account, deletes the file and the link stops working.

6. Reports

A report is the one record that is not erased when an account goes.

When you report somebody we keep the category you chose, the specific reason, anything you typed, and the time. We keep who reported whom, because a single complaint and a fifth complaint about the same account are not the same thing, and because one person reporting fifteen profiles in an afternoon is itself worth seeing. The person you report is never told who reported them, and reporting them removes them from your deck for good.

If either of you later deletes their account, the report survives with the link to the deleted account severed — the reason and the text stay readable, attached to nobody. We keep it because deleting an account is a common thing to do after being reported, and a safety record that disappears on request is not a safety record.

10. Your rights

  • Correct anything wrong (Art. 16). Most of it you can correct yourself from your profile, which is faster.
  • Delete your data (Art. 17). You can do this yourself — see deleting your account. The one exception is a report, explained in section 6.
  • Restrict or object to processing we do on legitimate interests (Art. 18 and 21).
  • Withdraw consent at any time, by clearing a special category field or turning notifications off. Withdrawing does not undo what was lawful before it.

Write to [[EMAIL]] and we will answer within one month. We may need to confirm you control the account before we hand over a copy of its data.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Αρχή Προστασίας Δεδομένων Προσωπικού Χαρακτήρα), www.dpa.gr, or to the authority where you live.

11. Automated decisions

We make no decision about you by automated means that has a legal or similarly significant effect, so Article 22 does not apply. The order of your swipe deck is decided automatically, and we describe exactly how in how our recommender system works — but being shown one profile before another is not a decision about you.

Suspending or removing an account is a human decision, informed by reports.

12. Security

Sign-in and password storage are handled by Firebase Authentication and we never hold your password. Every request to our API carries a verified identity token; the API takes your identity only from that token and never from the body of a request, so no client can claim to be somebody else. Firebase App Check means our API ignores requests that do not come from the real app. All traffic is over HTTPS. The database is not reachable from the public internet.

No system is perfect. If we ever suffer a breach that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell the Hellenic Data Protection Authority within 72 hours and tell you without undue delay.

13. Nobody under 18

FoitiFinder is for adults. The sign-up form refuses a date of birth under 18, the server refuses one too, and an account needs a verified university email address. If you believe an account belongs to a minor, report it from the profile — the "Underage" category exists for exactly this and goes to the front of the queue.
