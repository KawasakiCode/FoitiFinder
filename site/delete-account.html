Delete your account

You can delete your FoitiFinder account yourself, from inside the app, in under a minute. Nothing is held back and nobody has to approve it.

Last updated 5 November 2026.

From the app

  1. Open FoitiFinder and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Open Account settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm it is you, and confirm.

That is the whole process. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone — there is no grace period and no way for us to bring an account back.

Without the app

If you have uninstalled FoitiFinder or cannot sign in, email [[EMAIL]] from the university address you signed up with and ask us to delete the account. We will do it within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

We will ask you to prove you control the account before we delete it — writing from the registered address is usually enough. We do this because somebody else asking us to delete your account is the thing we most need to refuse.

What is deleted

All of it:

  • Your profile — name, date of birth, bio, gender, pronouns, height, orientation, religion, political view, smoking, drinking, intentions, children, course, job, volunteering, city, interests, traits and languages.
  • Your photos, including the image files themselves and their captions. The links stop working.
  • Your Instagram handle.
  • Every swipe you have made, in both directions — the likes and super likes you sent, and the ones you received.
  • Every match you are part of. It disappears from the other person's list as well, since a match needs two accounts to exist.
  • Your notifications, and your settings.
  • Your sign-in record at Firebase: your email address and your password hash.
  • Your notification token, so nothing can be pushed to your phone again.

Once this is done we hold nothing that identifies you, and the same email address can be used to sign up again from scratch.

What is kept, and why

One thing, and we would rather tell you now than have you find out later.

Reports. If you reported somebody, or somebody reported you, that report stays on file — the category, the reason, anything that was typed, and the date. The link to your account is severed, so what remains is attached to nobody and cannot be traced back to you by reading it.

We keep it because deleting an account is a common thing to do after being reported, and a safety record that vanishes on request protects the wrong person. Reports are kept for up to 3 years, or longer where they relate to a safety or legal matter that is still open. The Privacy Statement goes into more detail.

Beyond that, our hosting provider's operational request logs — which include IP addresses — age out on their own schedule, a matter of days. We do not keep copies.

Two things deleting will not do

  • It cannot take back an Instagram handle you already shared. If you confirmed a match, the other person has your handle and they keep it. Nothing in any app can reach into somebody's phone and remove what they have already read. If you want them to stop contacting you, block them on Instagram — and if they have done something worth reporting, report them before you delete your account, because afterwards there is nothing for us to attach the report to.
  • It does not cancel anything you bought. Today there is nothing to buy, so this is moot — see the Cancellation Policy.

If you want less than deletion

You do not have to delete your account to get out of the way:

  • Turn match notifications off in settings and the app will stop reaching you.
  • Clear any field you regret from your profile. Your orientation, religion and political view are optional and emptying one removes it from our database.
  • Remove a photo and the file goes with it.
  • Ask for a copy of your data instead, at [[EMAIL]], before you go. Easier to ask while the account still exists.
