3. What the service actually is

So there is no confusion about what you are signing up for:

  • You are shown profiles one at a time and you like, pass or super like each one. You get a limited number of super likes in any 7-day period.
  • A like is not shown to the person who received it. A super like is.
  • When two people like each other a match is created. Nothing is exchanged yet.
  • Each of you must then confirm the match. When the second confirmation lands — and not a moment before — each of you receives the other's Instagram handle. Either of you can decline instead, and nothing is exchanged.
  • A match nobody has finished answering expires 3 days after it was created, or 3 days after the first confirmation. It is then deleted and leaves both of your lists. After that it is over, and you will not be shown each other again.
  • There is no messaging inside FoitiFinder. Whatever happens after the handles are exchanged happens on Instagram or elsewhere, where we can see nothing and control nothing. That does not mean we will ignore a report about it — see section 8 — only that we are not a party to it.

7. The Instagram handle

This part is worth its own section because it is the one thing in the app that cannot be undone.

Handing over your handle is a decision you make twice: once by liking, and once by confirming. Once it has been shown to somebody, it cannot be taken back. Removing the match clears it from your list, which is for your own comfort — the other person keeps what they have already seen. Decide accordingly, and treat somebody else's handle the same way you would want yours treated: do not publish it, pass it on, or use it for anything other than talking to that person.

Because the handle outlives the match, so does your ability to report the person who has it. Everybody whose match you removed is listed by name under Account settings → Matches you removed, and you can report any of them from there at any time — see the Guidelines.

What happens on Instagram is between you and them, and subject to Instagram's own rules, not ours.
